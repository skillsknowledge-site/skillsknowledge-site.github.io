Terms of use

By creating an account you agree to use SkillsKnowledge honestly. Do not send abusive, unlawful or misleading messages, do not try to break into other accounts, and do not copy or resell the work shown on this website without permission.

An account can be blocked if these rules are broken. A blocked person can send an appeal from the appeal page, and it will be read by a person.

Orders and projects are agreed with the owner before work starts. The website is provided as it is, and the owner may change or remove features at any time.

Points and reference codes

Points are a reward for inviting people you know. They are not money, cannot be sold or moved to another person, and can only be used to lower the price of an order. Every point expires after a set time (7 days at level 0, longer at higher levels).

To use Points you need a confirmed email address and a phone number that we have checked. If you change your phone number, it must be checked again. Points can be earned from reference codes, from paid orders (5 points for every started 1,000 PKR) and when the owner adds them by hand. Your level starts at 0 and grows with your paid orders. If an order is cancelled, the unused points from it are taken back.

The Points page opens a short time after you create an account. A person who joins through a reference link must approve that reference before using the website.

A reference code lasts 24 hours and can be used by 3 people. Each person may make up to 8 codes in every 30-day period, counted from the day they joined. The limit can grow with paid orders. If you reach the limit, you cannot make another code until the period ends. Trying to make a code after reaching the limit blocks your access to Points automatically.

You may not use your own link, fake or duplicate accounts, or any trick to collect points. Prices shown in other currencies are a guide only; payment is taken in PKR. Points from such accounts or from codes made beyond the limit are not paid.

Blocks work like this. Points can be blocked for a short time by the owner while a case is checked (code 2XC5T), and the time left on your active code is paused until you are unblocked. A block for trying after the limit (code 3CFYO) is lifted only after you send an appeal and it is accepted, and then Points stay closed for 60 days. If it happens a second time, your whole account is terminated (code 5PB60) with the reason shown, and if the owner accepts your appeal you can enter the website again only after 60 days.

You may send one Points appeal every 24 hours from the Points page. Appeals are read by a person within 2 to 3 days. The owner may change these rules and the point values at any time.

Payments

Card and Google Pay payments are handled on the secure page of our payment company. Your card details are typed there only. We, our website, our database and our admin never see or store them. Prices are set in Pakistani rupees; if you pay in another currency, the amount is converted at the market rate when you pay. Your order is marked paid automatically when the payment company confirms the money.

Privacy policy

We keep the details you give when you sign up: your name, email, username and, if you add it, your phone number. They are used to run your account, answer your messages and handle your orders. They are not sold.

The website counts visits so the owner can see how many people come. A random number is kept in your browser for this. It holds no name, email or other personal detail.

Comments, complaints and files you send through the feedback form are visible to the owner and the admins. You can ask for your account and its data to be deleted from your profile page.
